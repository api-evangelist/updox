Fax is not the relic some people assume it to be. In healthcare, it remains a practical bridge between systems that still do not communicate. Providers, labs, payers, specialists, pharmacies, and medical billing partners all operate across mixed platforms, and they need a reliable way to exchange sensitive documents.
